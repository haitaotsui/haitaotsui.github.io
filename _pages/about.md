Haitao Xu is a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Cleveland State University (advisor:[Dr. Jingru Zhang](https://zhangatcs.github.io)), Cleveland, Ohio. He received his M.S. degree in Software Engineering from Kunming University of Science and Technology, Kunming, China. His research interests include algorithms and data structures, combinatorial optimization, and operations research. Besides, He worked as a software engineer for over five years, gaining experience in requirements analysis, system design, application development, maintenance, and system programming. Working on projects ranging from web applications to backend services.
