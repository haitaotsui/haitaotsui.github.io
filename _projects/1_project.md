Developed a video conferencing product consisting of both ALL-IN-ONE Terminal and Software Terminal(similar to Zoom).
- 1.Built cross-platform components (Windows, Android, iOS, Linux), enabling shared functionality and reducing redundant development.
- 2.Extended an XMPP-based instant messaging module to support reliable, real-time communication, enhancing messaging stability and user experience. (similar to WhatsApp).
- 3.Designed reusable APIs and optimized data exchange with Protobuf, improving integration efficiency and cross-module performance.
- 4.Implemented WebRTC-based conferencing features, delivering scalable, high-quality, low-latency video calls.
